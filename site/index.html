Find files by what they mean.

Press Ctrl Alt Space, describe what you're looking for in your own words, and get the file, even when it's written in another language and named scan_0042.pdf. Documents, photos, audio and video: "where did we negotiate the deposit?" finds the e-mail and the lease, "a red car in the snow" finds the picture, "the meeting about the budget" finds the recording.

Not just documents: photos, video and audio too

Valafind looks at your pictures, watches your videos and listens to your recordings, so you can find them by what is in them. "our dog playing on the beach" finds the photo, the video and the voice memo from that day, even though they're called IMG_20250712_163045.jpg, VID_20250712_170211.mp4 and Recording 2025-07-12 16.58.m4a.

No tags, albums or file names needed: describe it the way you remember it.

Documents, photos, audio and video

Valafind reads your documents, PDFs, slides, spreadsheets, e-mail and code, looks at your photos, and listens to your recordings and videos, and understands them with EmbeddingGemma 2, in 100+ languages. Ask the way you'd ask a colleague.

Your everyday files first

It starts with the files you actually use (your recent and frequent documents) and then works through the rest of your home folder. New and changed files are picked up as they happen.

Stays out of your way

Indexing uses the CPU and disk your computer isn't using, at background priority, and steps aside the moment you search. When everything is indexed, Valafind just shows a quiet ✓.

Busy? It slows down.

Compiling, gaming, on a call: Valafind measures the load every two seconds and turns its own speed up or down to keep the whole machine just under your limits. Quiet machine, many files at once; busy machine, a trickle. The tick on each meter shows the target.

You choose the limits: maximum CPU and disk use, which folders to index, and whether to pause on battery.

Nothing leaves your computer.

The AI model runs locally and ships with the app: no cloud, no account, no telemetry. Your files, your index and your searches never leave your computer, and the index is stored in your user folder, readable only by you. The only network request Valafind makes is checking GitHub for a newer version every few hours, which you can turn off in Settings.

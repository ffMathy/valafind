Meaning, not file names

Valafind reads your documents, PDFs, slides, spreadsheets, e-mail and code, looks at your photos, and listens to your recordings and videos, and understands them with EmbeddingGemma 2, in 100+ languages. Ask the way you'd ask a colleague.

Your everyday files first

It starts with the files you actually use (your recent and frequent documents) and then works through the rest of your home folder. New and changed files are picked up as they happen.

Stays out of your way

Indexing only runs while your CPU and disk are idle, at background priority, and steps aside the moment you search. When everything is indexed, Valafind just shows a quiet ✓.

Busy? It waits.

Compiling, gaming, on a call: Valafind notices the load and pauses. It picks up again when the machine is quiet, or right away if you press Index Now.

You choose the limits: maximum CPU and disk use, which folders to index, and whether to pause on battery.

Nothing leaves your computer.

The AI model runs locally and ships with the app: no cloud, no account, no telemetry. Your files, your index and your searches never leave your computer, and the index is stored in your user folder, readable only by you. The only network request Valafind makes is checking GitHub for a newer version every few hours, which you can turn off in Settings.
